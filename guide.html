1 · What this is and how to start ／ 這是什麼、怎麼開始

A clickable demo of an EMR with a Smart MDT module. Two stories are prepared: Route A — a breast-cancer case goes from a specialist consult to the weekly team meeting (MDT), the locked record and the hand-off tasks; Route B — a difficult-discharge case goes from allied-health consults to a case conference and a family meeting. A guided tour walks you through both, one step at a time.

  • Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop (a wide screen helps).
  • Everything you do is saved only in this browser. Another browser or computer starts fresh.
  • Language: EN / 中文 at the top right. The tour and all button names follow the language you choose.
  • There are no passwords. The tour switches roles for you.

3 · Demo time (the demo clock) ／ 示範時間（示範時鐘）

The demo does not use your computer’s clock. It has its own clock that starts at 2026-11-04 09:40, the morning before the breast team meets at 14:00. Anything that depends on time — starting the meeting, holding the case conference, the family meeting, a report coming back — only becomes possible once the demo clock has reached that moment. That is why some buttons do not exist until you move the clock.

How to move it: at the top of every MDT page there is a grey, collapsed line “Demo controls · demo time 2026-11-04 09:40”. Click it to expand, then press a jump button:

  • MDT meeting · 11-04 14:00 — route A, before “Start the meeting” (step 8).
  • Case conference · 11-05 07:50 and Family meeting · 11-05 13:30 — route B, if a record or signature button is not offered yet.
  • Treatment day · 11-06 09:30 — optional, to see the chemotherapy administration day.
  • Receive echo report (LVEF 60%) · 11-05 08:50 — optional, returns the cardiac report so Margaret’s readiness becomes 8/8.

The clock only moves forward; a jump button turns grey once that time has passed. “Restart demo data” brings the clock back to the start. Any role can use the demo controls; they are demo tools, not product functions.

demo 不用你電腦的時間，它有自己的時鐘，從 2026-11-04 09:40 開始，也就是乳癌團隊 14:00 開會那天的早上。所有跟時間有關的事：開始會議、召開個案會議、家庭會議、報告回來，都要等示範時鐘走到那個時間才能做。所以有些按鈕在你調時鐘之前是不會出現的。

怎麼調：每個 MDT 頁面最上方有一行灰色、收合的 「示範控制 · 示範時間 2026-11-04 09:40」。點它展開，再按跳轉按鈕：

  • MDT 會議 · 11-04 14:00：A 線，按「開始會議」之前（第 8 步）。
  • 個案會議 · 11-05 07:50、家庭會議 · 11-05 13:30：B 線，如果紀錄或簽名的按鈕還沒出現時用。
  • 治療日 · 11-06 09:30：可選，看化療給藥日。
  • 收到心臟超音波報告（LVEF 60%）· 11-05 08:50：可選，讓心臟報告回來，Margaret 的資料齊備變 8/8。

時鐘只能往前；過了的時間按鈕會變灰。「重置示範資料」會把時鐘帶回起點。任何身分都能用示範控制；它是示範工具，不是產品功能。

Limits

  • AI assistance, remote MDT, automatic enrolment and PACS images are listed only as roadmap.
  • EN and 中文 only; no Vietnamese strings for the MDT module.

Sending feedback

Open the Feedback panel → All feedback → Export JSON or Export CSV (or Copy as text) and send us the file or text. It contains only your verdicts and notes — no patient data.
