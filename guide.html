EMR · Smart MDT demo — one-page guide ／ 一頁操作說明

Demo Hospital · Oncology · pure front-end, no server · all patients, staff and data are fictitious ／ 純前端、無伺服器，所有病人、人員、資料全部虛構

1 · Open the demo ／ 開啟

  • Double-click index.html (works from a folder or USB stick), or serve the demo folder with any static server.
  • Language: EN / 中文 at the top right. Default English.
  • Roles: use the account menu (top right) → Switch role, or put actor=<key> in the URL. No passwords.
  • Start the guided tour: index.html?tour=1 — a panel at the bottom left walks through both routes step by step; the panel at the bottom right collects your feedback per page.
  • 雙擊 index.html 即可開（資料夾或隨身碟皆可），或用任何靜態伺服器掛 demo 資料夾。
  • 右上角切換 EN／中文，預設英文。
  • 身分：右上角帳號選單 →「Switch role」，或在網址加 actor=<帳號鍵>；沒有密碼。
  • 導覽模式：index.html?tour=1 — 左下角面板逐步帶看兩條故事線；右下角面板收集每一頁的回饋。

5 · Terms, controls and limits ／ 用詞、示範控制、限制

Six fixed terms

Specialist consult專科照會 — doctor to doctor, consultant can order
Allied-health consult職類照會 — nutrition, pharmacy, rehabilitation, social work; template-based, orders only after the attending adopts
Multi-physician consultation院內會診 — several attendees, chair and secretary; read-only records in this demo
Team meeting (MDT)團隊會議 MDT — standing tumour board with weekly agenda
Case conference個案跨團隊會議 — started by the attending from the team overview
Family meeting家庭會議 — separate record with role-based signatures

Demo controls (MDT page, top)

Jump the demo clock (meeting time, case conference, family meeting, treatment day) or return the echocardiography report. Any role can use them; they are demo tools, not product functions. “Restart demo data” in the tour panel resets this browser’s demo state (feedback is kept).

Limits

  • All data is fictitious; nothing is sent anywhere (no network calls).
  • MDT write actions are for demonstration only and are not part of the phase 1 scope.
  • AI assistance, remote MDT, automatic enrolment and PACS images are listed only as roadmap.
  • Vietnamese interface strings for the MDT module are not included; EN and 中文 only.

六個固定用詞

專科照會醫師對醫師，受照會醫師可開醫囑
職類照會營養、藥學、復健、社工；範本式，建議經主治採納才成醫囑
院內會診多人出席、有主席與秘書；demo 只展示紀錄
團隊會議 MDT常設腫瘤團隊，每週議程
個案跨團隊會議由主治從團隊總覽啟動
家庭會議另立紀錄，依角色簽名

示範控制（MDT 頁頂）

跳示範時鐘（開會、個案會議、家庭會議、治療日）或讓心臟超音波報告回來；任何身分都能按；是示範工具，不是功能。導覽面板的「Restart demo data」會重置這台瀏覽器的示範狀態（回饋保留）。

限制

  • 資料全部虛構；不對外傳送任何資料（沒有網路呼叫）。
  • MDT 寫入功能僅供示範，不代表一期交付範圍。
  • AI 輔助、遠距 MDT、自動排入與 PACS 影像只列為 roadmap。
  • MDT 模組沒有越南文字串；只有 EN 與中文。

6 · Feedback ／ 回饋

On every page, the Feedback panel (bottom right) lets you mark the page Fits our workflow Needs change Not needed and leave a note. “All feedback” lists everything saved in this browser; Export JSON / CSV or Copy as text and send it back to us. The export holds only your verdicts and notes — no patient data.

每一頁右下角的 Feedback 面板可對該頁標記 符合 要改 不需要 並留意見。「All feedback」列出這台瀏覽器存的全部回饋；用 Export JSON／CSV 或 Copy as text 寄回給我們。匯出只含您的判斷與意見，沒有病人資料。
